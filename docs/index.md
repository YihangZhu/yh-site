Yihang Zhu received his master's degree in Electrical Engineering at Shenyang
Jianzhu University, China in 2016. He is currently doing his PhD supervised by
[Prof. Huiyu Zhou](https://le.ac.uk/people/huiyu-zhou) and
[Prof. Rajeev Raman](https://le.ac.uk/people/rajeev-raman) in the School of
Computing and Mathematical Sciences at the University of Leicester.

His main research interests include machine learning, computer vision and
combinatorial optimization.
